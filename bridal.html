How far ahead should I book?

Three to four months for wedding season (November to February), six weeks otherwise. Your date is held once the deposit is in.

How does the deposit work?

30% confirms your dates, paid by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. The rest is due on the first event day.

Can you come to the venue?

Yes, anywhere in Karachi, for a travel fee of Rs 8,000 per event. Most brides prefer the lounge for the lighting and calm.

What should I bring to the trial?

Your outfit or a photo of it, your jewellery, and any looks you love. We'll photograph the result for you to keep.
